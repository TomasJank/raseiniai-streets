Car sound audition

Every sound the carsound step adds to the game. Use headphones or decent speakers: an engine loop is mostly below 300 Hz. The page runs the game's own crossfade code.

What to listen for

  1. Each loop on its own: a click or a thump once a second or so means a bad seam.
  2. Drag the rpm slider slowly from 850 to 5600, with and without throttle: the sound should rise smoothly with no jumps in character or loudness where one loop hands over to the next.
  3. The simulated drive: three gear changes (the rpm falls at each), a steady cruise, then braking to idle.
  4. Which petrol engine you prefer. Changing it in the game is one line: const CS_PICK={petrol:'p',diesel:'d'} at the top of pipeline/carsound.js (q = Peugeot 404).

Other car sounds

Horn: a 2008 Fiat Panda; squeal: a Chrysler LHS cornering; siren: a Whelen wail (the game pans it towards the nearest police car). The speed bump is what carBumpSound(strength) plays, pitched up and louder for harder bumps.
